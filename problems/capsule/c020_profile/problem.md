# C020 · Checkpoint: a profile

The input has two lines: a name (one word) and an age. Print three lines:

```
Hello, NAME! You are AGE.
In 10 years you will be AGE10.
You were born in YEAR or YEAR1.
```

where AGE10 is the age plus 10, YEAR is 2026 minus the age and YEAR1 is one less. Keep 2026 in a constant.

**Example**

Input (also in `in.txt`):
```
Ada
36
```
Output:
```
Hello, Ada! You are 36.
In 10 years you will be 46.
You were born in 1990 or 1989.
```

**Focus:** checkpoint for module 2: reading text and numbers, variables, a constant and calculated values.
